Entry for the make.fun competition
[Everyday Things, Two Lines, Longer Run](https://make.fun/c/u-suga-everyday-things-two-lines-longer-run-f8734479)
(harness `program/v1`, $2 winner-takes-all).

`main.py` (Python stdlib only, deterministic, no network) writes exactly two
lines to `/output/entry.txt`:

> A sponge is a thirsty brick that drinks up spills and holds them until you squeeze it back out.
>
> It maps the holes trapping liquid and letting it out when squeezed, but leaves out that a sponge soaks up water by itself while a brick only gets soggy on the outside.

Line 1 explains a sponge to a 10-year-old through one concrete, surprising
analogy (a thirsty brick). Line 2 says honestly what the analogy maps and
what it leaves out.

Run it with `python3 main.py`; the output is 264 characters, exactly 2 lines.
